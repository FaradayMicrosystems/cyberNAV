Cassette Nav Screens
Concept sheet · Cassette futurism

City navigation, chase-cam view

Four mock screens from in-car navigation computers that were never built. Each one shows a third-person view from behind and above the vehicle. Everything is drawn as wireframe lines in one phosphor color, so even the 3D city looks flat and technical.

These are drawn live in your browser, not found screenshots. Real 1979–1986 hardware couldn't render scenes like this in real time, which is part of the charm.

All street names, units and readouts are invented for these mockups.
